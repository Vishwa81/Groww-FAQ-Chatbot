# Regulatory & Product Disclaimer

**Groww Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant**

> **"Facts-only. No investment advice."**

---

### Purpose
The Groww Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant is an AI-powered conversational prototype developed exclusively for product research and educational purposes as part of a product management fellowship milestone.

### Non-Advisory Nature
1. **Informational Utility Only**: The Assistant is restricted to retrieving and summarizing factual information published in official Scheme Information Documents (SID), Key Information Memorandums (KIM), and official platform service documentation.
2. **Not a Financial Advisor**: This application is **NOT** a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (RIA), Research Analyst, or Portfolio Manager. It does not provide financial planning, portfolio suitability assessments, investment recommendations, buy/sell/hold calls, or return forecasts.
3. **No Commercial Solicitation**: Content presented through this prototype does not constitute an offer, solicitation, or recommendation to buy or sell units in any mutual fund scheme.

### Market Risk Disclosure
Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Please read all scheme-related documents (SID, KIM, and Addenda) carefully before investing. Past performance is not an indicator of future returns.

### Verification of Data
While responses strive to reflect official disclosures accurately once the RAG pipeline is fully connected, users must verify all financial parameters (such as NAV, expense ratios, exit loads, and tax provisions) directly against official AMC disclosures and SEBI guidelines.
