HDFC MUTUAL FUND - OFFICIAL STATEMENT & CAS DOWNLOAD GUIDE
Published by: HDFC Asset Management Company Limited
Effective Date: November 01, 2026 | Document Type: Official Service Guide
Official Portal: https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/consolidated-account-statement

1. OVERVIEW OF MUTUAL FUND ACCOUNT STATEMENTS
An Account Statement (AS) is an official consolidated document that provides a detailed summary of all mutual fund transactions and folio holdings for an investor. It includes purchases, redemptions, switches, systematic transactions (SIP/STP/SWP), and dividend/IDCW distributions. There is no charge or fee for generating or downloading your account statement.

2. HOW TO DOWNLOAD YOUR HDFC MUTUAL FUND ACCOUNT STATEMENT ONLINE
Investors can download or receive their account statement directly via the official HDFC Mutual Fund portal by following these steps:

Step 1: Visit the official HDFC Mutual Fund Services portal at:
https://www.hdfcfund.com/services/consolidated-account-statement

Step 2: Enter your Identifier:
- Select PAN (Permanent Account Number) to receive a consolidated statement of all folios linked to your PAN, OR
- Select Folio Number to receive a statement specific to a single folio.

Step 3: One-Time Password (OTP) Authentication:
Click 'Send OTP' to receive a secure verification code on your registered mobile number and email ID. Enter the OTP to authenticate.

Step 4: Select the Statement Duration:
Choose from:
- Current Financial Year
- Previous Financial Year
- Full Statement (since inception)
- Select Period (custom date range)

Step 5: Receive or Download Statement:
Click 'SEND EMAIL' to receive your password-protected PDF statement immediately at your registered email address, or download it directly from the investor portal.

3. CONSOLIDATED ACCOUNT STATEMENT (CAS) ACROSS ALL FUND HOUSES
If you hold investments across multiple mutual funds (including HDFC and other AMCs):
- CAMS CAS Portal: Investors can request an e-CAS covering all CAMS and KFintech serviced funds via https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Consolidated-Account-Statement.
- MFCentral Platform: Investors can view and download unified statements at https://www.mfcentral.com using PAN and mobile OTP authentication.
